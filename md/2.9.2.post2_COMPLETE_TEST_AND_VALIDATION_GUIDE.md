## 9. CUTLASS 4.6.2 Submodule Integration Analysis

### 9.1 Background and Motivation

- **CUTLASS Upgrade:** The submodule `csrc/cutlass` was updated to official release `v4.6.2` (commit `6c65a175668952f09bcbf66cb97a8de1b734b4a0`).
- **Header Alignment:** FlashAttention-2's CUDA kernels (`csrc/flash_attn`) rely on CUTLASS template headers (`csrc/cutlass/include/cutlass/*`). Aligning the submodule to v4.6.2 synchronizes header definitions with modern upstream requirements (including CuTe DSL compatibility).
- **Compilation Hygiene:** Under MSVC, previous compilation artifacts in `build/` can persist and cause partial link errors or stale `.obj` reuse. A full purge of `build/` ensured that all translation units were compiled strictly against CUTLASS 4.6.2 headers.

### 9.2 Verification Confirmation

1. Header verification: `csrc/cutlass/include/cutlass/version.h` confirms `#define CUTLASS_MAJOR 4`, `#define CUTLASS_MINOR 6`, `#define CUTLASS_PATCH 2`.
2. Binary integrity: The compiled binary `flash_attn_2_cuda.cp314-win_amd64.pyd` links cleanly, loads dynamically without missing symbols, and passes all 15 numerical accuracy cases and 7 functional API paths.
