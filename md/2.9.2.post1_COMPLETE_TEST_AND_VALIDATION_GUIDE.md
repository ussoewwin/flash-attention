## 9. A-1: Delta 1 + Delta 5 + Delta 6 Analysis

### 9.1 What changed from 2.9.0

| Aspect | 2.9.0 (original A-1) | 2.9.2.post1 (Delta 1/5/6) |
|---|---|---|
| Template type | `bool Use_rescale_threshold` | `float RescaleThreshold=0.0f` |
| Threshold value | `kSoftmaxRescaleSkipThreshold = -0.01f` | `RescaleThreshold = 8.0f` (at call sites) |
| Skip condition | `scaled_diff >= -0.01` | `scaled_diff >= -8.0` |
| Skip semantics | Use new max, approximate (0.7% error) | **FA4-style max lagging**: revert row_max to old value, **exact** |

### 9.2 Why the threshold changed (8.0f)

FA4 on B200 uses `rescale_threshold = 8.0 if q_dtype.width == 16 else 0.0`. This was validated at scale on the FA4 CI pipeline.

### 9.3 Why max lagging is required

At threshold 0.01, skipping the rescale but keeping the new max introduces bounded error (0.7%). At threshold 8.0, the same approach would produce up to ~256x overweighting of stale block contributions. Delta 6 implements FA4-style max lagging: on skip, revert `row_max(mi)` to `scores_max_prev(mi)`, making the output and LSE **mathematically exact**.

### 9.4 Accuracy impact of max lagging

The 18-case sweep (??4?5) plus 10 supplementary edge cases (??8) show zero accuracy regression. Worst fp16 rel_Linf = 7.94e-04; worst bf16 rel_Linf = 6.54e-03 (both well inside gates).

### 9.5 P-saturation safety

With max lagging, P can reach `exp2(8) = 256` before fp16/bf16 conversion. fp16 max = 65504; bf16 max = 3.39e38. No overflow risk.

## 10. A-2: Delta 4 Analysis + SASS Gate

### 10.1 What changed

In `utils.h`, `fma_f32x2`: `asm volatile(` ? `asm(`. The compiler is free to schedule the packed FMA.

### 10.2 A-2 accuracy

A-2's `fma_f32x2` path is active on sm_100+. All 28 accuracy cases (18 sweep + 10 edge) exercise A-2 with zero failures.

### 10.3 SASS gate: sm_120 result

**All 96 sm_120 cubins** checked for `FFMA.X2`:

- **FFMA.X2: NOT FOUND**
- Instead: `FFMA` (scalar) 5,760 occurrences, `FMUL.FTZ` 12,854 occurrences, `MUFU.EX2` 6,862 occurrences

**Interpretation:** On sm_120 (Blackwell), `fma.rn.f32x2` PTX may be lowered to scalar FFMA pairs by `ptxas`. The `check_sass_gates.py` tool was designed for sm_90 (Hopper) where `FFMA.X2` is the native encoding.

**Impact on correctness:** None. All accuracy tests pass. **Action:** SASS gate checker needs sm_120-specific instruction pattern update.

### 10.4 Determinism confirmation

The determinism test (??8.2) confirms `bit-identical = True` across two runs. The `volatile` removal (Delta 4) did not introduce non-deterministic behavior.
